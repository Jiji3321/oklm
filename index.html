About

The project

OKLM is an independent online multiplayer roleplay game inspired by the universe of Sword Art Online, developed on the Nanos World engine. The project is in an early stage of development.

Genre

Online multiplayer RPG

Platform

PC

Business model

Free to play. Optional cosmetics and VIP ranks, with no gameplay advantage.
